FAQ

Where is my data stored?

Only in this browser (local storage). Nothing is uploaded. Clearing browser data deletes your CVs, so export a PDF of anything important. PDF export needs an internet connection to load the PDF library; Print → Save as PDF is an alternative.

Is the AI real?

No. The assistant runs in an offline demo mode with rule-based suggestions. No AI service is connected.

Does the ATS score guarantee anything?

No. It is an estimate based on common resume checks, not a guarantee of passing any employer’s system.
